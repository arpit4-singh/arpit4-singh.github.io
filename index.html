Projects

Heart Disease Prediction

Heart disease is one of the world's leading causes of death, and early risk screening can help doctors act sooner. This app takes patient health details and predicts the risk of heart disease in real time.

I prepared the data, selected features, split it for training and testing, and compared models by accuracy score. The Streamlit interface adds charts and a live prediction form.

Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Streamlit
GitHub

Customer Segmentation (K-Means)

Businesses use customer segments to target offers, pricing and loyalty plans. This app groups customers by age, income and online spending, and names each group: Budget, Regular, Premium, VIP, Luxury and Elite.

You can change the number of clusters and see cluster centers and customer spread on a scatter plot.

Python, K-Means, Matplotlib, Streamlit
GitHub
